# Privacy Policy

**Telegram Share Extension** does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The extension only stores the Telegram Bot API token, chat ID, and user preferences locally in your browser using Chrome's `storage.local`.

Information is sent to Telegram servers only in these situations:
- When you explicitly send content to your configured chat through Telegram's API.
- When you use bot/chat validation previews in settings (`getMe`, `getChat`, and optional `getFile` for chat photos).

We do not track your activity, access private data, or share information with third parties.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact the developer.
